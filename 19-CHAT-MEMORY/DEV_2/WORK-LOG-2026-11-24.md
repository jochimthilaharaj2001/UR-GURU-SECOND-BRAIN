## 2. Technical Decisions Made
- **Decision (Private Storage):** Stored teacher verification evidence outside `wwwroot` in `App_Data/PrivateStorage/` with server-generated opaque keys (`teachers/{teacherProfileId}/docs/{guid}.ext`) rather than client filenames.
- **Decision (Antiforgery):** Disabled antiforgery for `POST /api/v1/teachers/me/identity-documents` because authentication relies strictly on custom `Authorization: Bearer <token>` headers, and cookies are neither issued nor accepted.
