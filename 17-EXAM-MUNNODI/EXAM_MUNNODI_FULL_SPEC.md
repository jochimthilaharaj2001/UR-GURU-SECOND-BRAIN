# XACT — Current Scope and Specification Gateway (BRD v6.2.2)

**Status:** Controlled requirements reference; not signed. **Replaces** the 17 November Exam Munnodi document as an active specification. Historical original: [archived copy](ARCHIVED_EXAM_MUNNODI_FULL_SPEC_2026-11-17.md).

## Active curricular scope
Sri Lankan G.C.E. A/L Grades 12–13, Biological Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics) and Physical Science (Combined Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics). Tamil/Sinhala/English only where individual content and translation versions are academically approved. Commerce, Arts, Technology and O/L are not part of first release.

## Mandatory capabilities (BR-11, BR-18, BR-19, BR-20)
- Five-year source-paper inventory, subject/syllabus/topic mapping, rights and academic approval.
- Stable canonical question concepts, independently approved translations and versioned answer keys; immutable scoring snapshots.
- Approved past-paper practice, topic/difficulty/count/time-configurable mocks, insufficient-question handling and server-side scoring.
- Server-owned deadline, durable autosave, idempotent submission and per-student results/history/topic analytics.
- Approved teacher paper drafts, immutable versions, authorized multi-recipient assignments, separate attempts/results.
- Moderated teacher question explanation videos, rights evidence, processing states, signed playback and written fallback.
- Enforce student ownership, teacher authorized-class scope, reviewer approvals and audit trails.

## Explicitly NOT automatically approved
AI grading, automatic transcription/AI notes, ExamPilot, DataSmart, new streams, XACT pricing/subscriptions, content reproduction rights, recording rights or compensation. These require separate decisions.

## Engineering release controls
Use BRD v6.2.2 §§3, 8, 10–14, Appendix A and AC-16–26. Specific mark schemes, time presets, negative marking, answer reveal, limits and content inventory remain pending OD-07–13. Exam exceptions must preserve backend deadline authority; the earlier 10-minute teacher grace and offline timer preferences require policy reconciliation, not silent promotion to signed BRD.
